💡 Storage tips

🥶 Keep refrigerated foods cold and return them to the fridge promptly.
❄️ Freezing can extend storage time when food is frozen while still suitable for freezing.
📦 Keep food in suitable sealed packaging and follow the package's storage instructions.
⚠️ Harmful bacteria may not change food's smell or colour.
Love your food. Waste less. This website tracks package dates and general storage estimates; it cannot determine whether food is safe to eat.
